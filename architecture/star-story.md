# STAR Story

## Situation

Enterprises are moving from single AI assistants to many specialized agents across business domains.

## Task

Build a governed runtime that coordinates agents, controls tools, simulates risk, escalates unsafe actions, and audits decisions.

## Action

Created deterministic synthetic agents, registries, task routing, tool policies, handoffs, conflict arbitration, simulations, safety gates, approvals, audit logs, briefings, dashboards, APIs, tests, Docker, and CI.

## Result

Produced a reproducible flagship project demonstrating governed multi-agent orchestration and enterprise-safe autonomous workflow design.
