# Role-Specific Positioning

## AI Platform Engineer

Emphasize multi-agent orchestration, optional live-agent adapter, tool governance, guardrails, tracing, evaluations, and approval workflows.

## Data Platform Engineer

Emphasize synthetic data generation, deterministic pipelines, DuckDB warehouse outputs, scorecards, APIs, dashboards, tests, and documentation.

## Data Engineer

Emphasize generated datasets, reproducible pipelines, CSV/JSON/Markdown artifacts, validation commands, and local execution.

## MLOps Engineer

Emphasize evaluation harness, risk/confidence scoring, regression reports, safety scorecards, trace outputs, and operational monitoring patterns.

## AI Governance Engineer

Emphasize policy enforcement, least privilege, prompt injection containment, approval queue, audit logs, decision lineage, and red-team scenarios.

## Platform Engineer

Emphasize control-plane architecture, deterministic runtime, API/dashboard surfaces, CI validation, Docker support, and future production integration points.

## Technical Program / AI Infrastructure Adjacent

Emphasize business problem framing, cross-domain workflows, executive briefings, approval gates, risk tradeoffs, and production roadmap.
