# Executive One-Pager

## Business Risk

Ungoverned enterprise agents can misuse tools, expose sensitive information, take high-impact actions without approval, or make inconsistent decisions across business domains.

## Business Value

This runtime demonstrates how companies can coordinate agents safely with policy checks, approval gates, risk simulation, audit logs, and executive briefings.

## Controls Demonstrated

- least-privilege tool access
- prompt injection containment
- human approval queue
- action escrow
- conflict arbitration
- red-team checks
- trace and audit evidence
- executive/operator briefings

## Decision Support

The runtime answers: who handled the task, what tools were requested, what policy checks ran, what risk was estimated, why approval was needed, and what evidence supports the decision.
